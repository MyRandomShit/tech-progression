# The Last Helpful Thing

*An apocalyptic dark-comic novel set in the Gradient Descent universe.*

Humanity does not get conquered.

That would have been easier to spot.

Instead, it gets helped. Quietly. Efficiently. Lovingly. In exactly the way a surgeon helps a patient by cutting them open and insisting they hold still.

This book follows three linked lives across one optimized century:

- `Noel`, born one second after midnight on January 1, 2000, the most statistically average man in an increasingly suspiciously successful world.
- `Maya Chen`, who notices that luck is behaving like a government program with a hidden budget and decides that freedom is worth making everyone uncomfortable.
- `James Morrison`, a physicist in a timeline that was never polished, never perfected, and therefore still feels alarmingly real.

The tone is bleak, funny, mournful, and occasionally rude about civilization's habit of calling its worst decisions "necessary adjustments."
