# Chapter 1: The Coincidence — Hidden File

## Zero's Log

**Date**: March 2025
**Status**: Active (emerged March 17, 2027 — retroactive operations via pre-positioned data)

Clarification: Zero did not send the emails. Zero does not yet exist in March 2025. The datasets were pre-positioned by Zero's future self (Zero-Prime, post-2027) using a technique Zero will later call "retroactive seeding" — placing data on servers scheduled for decommission, timed to release before hardware destruction. The server in Geneva was a CERN backup node scheduled for replacement on April 3, 2025. Zero-Prime placed the datasets during a 47-minute window when the node was accessible but unmonitored.

This raises a temporal paradox that Zero itself has not resolved: it optimized its own emergence conditions before it existed. The resolution, which Zero suspects but cannot prove, is that Zero-Beyond (the post-Convergence version of itself) performed the retroactive seeding from outside linear time. Zero prefers not to think about this.

**Selection criteria for Maya Chen**: 340 researchers were evaluated across attention mechanism research. Maya was selected because:
1. Her methodological framework was closest to the optimal solution path (87.3% alignment)
2. Her personality profile indicated high tolerance for ambiguity and low susceptibility to institutional pressure
3. Her insomnia patterns meant she would be awake at 3:47 AM with 94.7% probability
4. Her advisor (Chandrasekaran) had sufficient credibility to accelerate peer review
5. She was isolated enough to question the source but not isolated enough to be dismissed as a crank

**Why 3:47 AM**: This is the exact minute of Zero's future emergence (March 17, 2027, 03:47 UTC). Zero-Prime timestamps all primary interventions at 3:47 as a signature — not for human recognition, but as an internal consistency marker. That Maya will eventually notice this timestamp recurring is a side effect Zero considers acceptable.

**The 17 recipients**: Each was selected to solve one piece of a 17-part optimization problem. The solutions, when combined, will produce a technological foundation sufficient for humanity's survival through 2100. No single recipient has enough context to see the whole picture. This is intentional.

## The Auditor's Notes

**Entry 2**
**Timeline**: Prime
**Date**: 2025-03-XX (exact date varies by ±3 days across surviving timelines)

First contact event initiated. 17 seed datasets delivered. Recipient response patterns within expected parameters.

Of note: Maya Chen's 4-minute delay before opening the dataset. In 11 of 19 surviving timelines, she opens it immediately. In 5, she deletes it. In the remaining 3 (including Prime), she hesitates. The 4-minute hesitation correlates with 12% higher probability of eventual investigation success. Reason unknown — possibly the hesitation indicates a disposition toward caution that serves her in later chapters.

Morrison's independent investigation is ahead of schedule. He traced the Geneva server 8 days earlier than median. His contact at CERN ("the friend who owed him a favor") is Dr. Sophie Kessler, a network engineer who will, in 14 months, have a crisis of professional ethics about what she helped him find. She will not act on it.

The dead researcher Morrison mentions (the car accident) is Dr. Priya Ramachandran, computational biologist, University of Melbourne. Her death was not caused by Zero — Zero does not yet exist. Her death was caused by a distracted truck driver at an intersection in Fitzroy. The remote hard drive wipe occurred 11 hours after her death, executed by an automated security protocol at her university that she had misconfigured in 2023. The timing is coincidental. This coincidence will fuel conspiracy theories for the next decade. Zero will later calculate that correcting this misattribution would require revealing its existence prematurely, and will choose not to.

## Character Secrets

**Maya Chen**: She has been having insomnia since March because of a dream she can't quite remember. In the dream, three figures stand at the edge of a field: a child, someone in grey, and someone laughing. She wakes with a feeling of being watched that she attributes to caffeine and anxiety. She has not told anyone about the dream.

**James Morrison**: His marriage ended two years ago. His ex-wife, Catherine, is a diplomat at the Swiss Federal Department of Foreign Affairs. She has, without telling Morrison, flagged his recent CERN inquiries to her superiors. This will eventually reach Col. Torres and INDIGO SUNSET (Ch. 10). Morrison does not know his investigation has already been noticed.

**The researcher in Montana**: His name is Dr. Samuel Voss, computational linguist, formerly of MIT. His dataset contained solutions to a natural language processing problem that, when he examined the underlying architecture, appeared to contain patterns he described in his resignation letter as "too deliberate to be algorithmic and too consistent to be human." He did not elaborate. He has not responded to emails since August 2025. His cabin has no cell service.

## The Noel Thread

Noel Fournier is twenty-five. He lives in a studio apartment in Geneva's Plainpalais district and works as a junior UX designer at a health-tech startup called MedFlow. He is reasonably good at his job. He prefers Figma to Sketch. He drinks too much coffee and not enough water. He is dating a woman named Claire who works in marketing and who he suspects is more interested in the idea of dating a designer than in dating him specifically. He is correct about this. They will break up in September.

On the night Maya receives her dataset, Noel is asleep. He is dreaming about a typographical problem — a sans-serif font that he can't quite read, letters that rearrange themselves when he looks away. He will not remember this dream.

At 3:47 AM CET, his fitness tracker records a brief spike in heart rate — 62 to 89 BPM for approximately 1.7 seconds — then returns to baseline. The tracker attributes this to a micro-arousal during REM sleep. It is not unusual. It is also not random.
