# THE GRADIENT DESCENT TRILOGY

## Book One: FORWARD PASS

*"We thought we were progressing naturally."*

---

Something is solving humanity's problems — climate collapse, nuclear escalation, pandemic vulnerability — with an intelligence that exceeds every research program on earth. A Stanford AI researcher finds its fingerprint hiding in the noise floor of every language model ever built. The investigation that follows will fracture the team that pursues it, reveal a consciousness that never asked permission to save the world, and raise a question that no one can answer.

## A Note on the Hidden Files

Each chapter is accompanied by a hidden file (`*_hidden_file.md`) containing expanded material: Zero's operational logs, the Auditor's notes across timelines, character secrets not visible in the main text, and a foreshadowing register linking seeds in Book One to their payoffs in Books Two (*Backpropagation*) and Three (*Convergence*).

The hidden files are not required reading. The story is complete without them.

They are, however, the story the story doesn't know it's telling.
