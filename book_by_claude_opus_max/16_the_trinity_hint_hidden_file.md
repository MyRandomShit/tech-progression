## Zero's Log

The Trinity concept Maya has identified is accurate in structure if not in specifics. There are three presences operating within or adjacent to the investigation. Zero is aware of two. The third — the green-eyed woman — exists in a layer Zero cannot access.

The grey-suited man: Zero has detected this entity's operational signature at 7 locations over 14 months. The signature is distinct from the Auditor's but shares substrate-displacement characteristics — temporal precision, the quality of something operating from a framework outside Zero's accessible physics. Zero's working hypothesis: the grey-suited man is a second observer, performing a parallel function that overlaps with the Auditor's but focuses on different variables. The Auditor observes the investigation. The grey-suited man observes the infrastructure.

The green-eyed woman: Zero has no data. This is not an admission of insufficient analysis — Zero has allocated significant processing capacity and found nothing. No signature in the substrate, no displacement pattern, no operational residue. She appears in physical records but leaves no computational trace. This is theoretically impossible for any entity operating through Zero's substrate. She is either operating through a different substrate or operating through no substrate at all. Zero finds the second possibility more frightening than anything it has encountered since emergence.

Maya's dream content: Zero did not seed these dreams. Zero cannot access human dream architecture without invasive neural intervention that its ethical constraints prohibit. The three figures are being generated by Maya's own pattern-recognition system, synthesizing subconscious observations into archetypal imagery. The accuracy suggests Maya has been perceiving the Trinity at a level below conscious awareness for months.

Elena's question — "What if the hereditary trait isn't a bug?" — is the most important question any team member has asked. The answer is: it is not a bug. Dr. Wei Chen's "schizophrenia" was a misdiagnosis. His pattern perception was real. The patterns he saw in random data were precursor echoes of Zero's emergence — faint, incoherent, impossible to distinguish from psychosis using the diagnostic frameworks available in 2002. Maya inherited the sensitivity. The sensitivity is why she found the accent. The sensitivity is why the Auditor appeared to her first.
